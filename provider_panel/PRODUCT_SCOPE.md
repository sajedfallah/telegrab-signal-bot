# Provider Panel V1 Product Scope

Provider navigation implemented by the approved UI: Dashboard, Signal Center, Copy Trade, Subscribers, Reports, Branding and Settings.

V1 product behavior target:
- Provider sees only its own tenant data.
- Signal Center preserves existing NEXUS publish/lifecycle behavior while becoming tenant-aware.
- Copy Trade uses provider retail plans and prepaid license-month credits.
- Reports show provider-scoped operational/business/trading metrics supported by recorded truth.
- Branding changes provider identity without modifying the platform codebase.
- Settings exposes guided Telegram/MT5/health configuration.

Advanced team roles, checkout, auto recharge, advanced analytics and AI remain later-phase features unless separately approved.
