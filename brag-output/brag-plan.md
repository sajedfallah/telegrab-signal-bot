# NEXUS Launch Video V1

## Goal
Create a short vertical launch video for the current NEXUS Telegram Mini App, based on the real UI in `feature/live-charts-v1`.

## Output
- Format: 9:16 vertical
- Target: 1080x1920
- Duration: ~22 seconds
- Voiceover: off for V1
- Style: dark, premium fintech, restrained motion, mobile-first readability
- Claims: no profit/performance claims

## Source of truth
- Mini App UI: `miniapp/`
- Landing: `miniapp/assets/brand/`
- Logo/mark: `nexus-logo.svg`, `nexus-mark.svg`
- Live Charts: `miniapp/live-charts.js`, `miniapp/live-charts.css`
- Product UI must remain visually faithful to the repository.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Brand / Landing (0:00–0:03)
Use the current NEXUS landing visual and brand mark.
Text: `NEXUS`
Subtext: `Trading Intelligence`
Motion: slow push-in + subtle cyan glow.

### Scene 2 — Enter NEXUS / Home (0:03–0:07)
Transition from the landing screen into the real Mini App Home.
Focus on top bar, NEXUS identity and dashboard depth.
Text overlay: `Your Trading Hub`

### Scene 3 — Signal Center (0:07–0:11)
Navigate to Signals.
Highlight Free and VIP signal experience without showing or implying guaranteed returns.
Text overlay: `Signals. Clear. Connected.`

### Scene 4 — Live Charts Hero (0:11–0:16)
Navigate to the Charts tab.
Show the real Live Charts interface.
Visual sequence: BTC -> XAU and 5m -> 15m.
Emphasize candlesticks, symbol selector, timeframe selector and live-data status.
Text overlay: `Live Market. One Screen.`

### Scene 5 — Plans / AutoTrade (0:16–0:19)
Navigate to Plans and briefly expose the AutoTrade product path.
Text overlay: `From Signal to Execution`
Do not fabricate an execution result or account profit.

### Scene 6 — Outro (0:19–0:22)
Return to NEXUS brand mark/logo on dark background.
Headline: `NEXUS`
Tagline: `Signals • Charts • Execution`
End with a clean glow decay.

## Visual language
- Background: deep navy/black from current NEXUS UI
- Accent: cyan/teal from current UI
- Typography: Vazirmatn for Persian UI; existing NEXUS Latin typography for brand text
- Rounded panels and restrained glow matching Mini App
- No generic crypto stock footage
- No fake dashboards or synthetic prices presented as real

## Editing rhythm
- 30 fps
- Fast enough for Reels/Telegram, but each UI scene must remain readable
- Prefer camera push, mask reveals and short cross-dissolves over flashy transitions
- UI is the hero; decorative motion stays secondary

## V1 acceptance criteria
1. Uses real NEXUS branding/assets.
2. Shows current five-item navigation including Charts.
3. Live Charts is a hero scene.
4. No unsupported profit or performance claim.
5. No fabricated Gold/Forex price data.
6. Mobile text remains readable at 9:16.
7. Final frame clearly identifies NEXUS.
