# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, technically honest, and professional. Focus criticism on code, designs, evidence, and behavior rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and knowingly submitting malicious or deceptive changes.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainer through the contact method documented in the repository.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related communication.
